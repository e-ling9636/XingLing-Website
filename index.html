About

Xing Ling is an Assistant Professor at the School of Statistics and Data Science, Southwestern University of Finance and Economics. Her research focuses on statistical hypothesis testing and multiple testing, particularly p-value combination methods under dependence.

Her research interests include the theoretical properties of statistical testing procedures and their applications to practical statistical problems.

I welcome opportunities for research collaboration.

Research

01

Hypothesis Testing

My research focuses on statistical hypothesis testing and multiple testing, with particular interest in p-value combination methods under dependence structures and their asymptotic theory.

I am also interested in how local testing evidence can be combined to provide both global inference and local diagnostic information.

Publications

Published

Multiple testing in rolling-window analysis via p-value combination

Xing Ling, Qian Wu, and Kun Chen

Economics Letters, 268 (2026), 113198

This study addresses multiple testing problems arising from overlapping rolling windows. It uses the harmonic mean p-value to construct global tests and window-level diagnostics, providing a framework for combining evidence across dependent testing procedures.
